Top Shelf: privacy, terms and refunds

Last updated November 12, 2026 (sign-in added). Top Shelf Hockey Trivia Game ("Top Shelf") is run by Alex Eriksen in Denmark. Contact: topshelfhockeytrivia@gmail.com.

Privacy policy

Top Shelf collects as little as it can. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What is stored in your browser. A random player ID (a long code that does not contain your name or email), your result for the day and, if you sign in, your sign-in session. This lets the game remember that you played and, if you buy it, recognise your purchase. It stays on your device until you clear your browser data.

What is stored on our servers.

  • For each question you answer: your random player ID, the date and the question. This stops the same player from being counted twice in a day.
  • Totals of how many players gave each answer. These totals are not linked to you.
  • If you sign in: your email address, kept by our sign-in provider (Supabase) so we can email you a one-time code and recognise your purchase on any device. Signed-in answers are recorded under your account ID instead of the random player ID.
  • If you buy unlimited mode: your account ID, the Stripe payment reference and the email address you gave at checkout, so we can unlock the mode and help you if something goes wrong.

Who processes data for us.

  • Supabase stores the game data above and handles sign-in.
  • An email delivery service sends your one-time sign-in code.
  • Stripe handles payment. We never see or store your card details.
  • GitHub hosts this website. Like any web host, it sees your IP address when you load the page.
  • Google Fonts supplies the game's typeface, so your browser contacts Google when the page loads.

How long we keep it. Game records are kept while the game runs. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to support your purchase and meet accounting rules.

Your rights. You can ask us to see, correct or delete the data tied to your player ID or purchase. If you are in the EU, UK or a similar region, you also have the rights set out in your local data protection law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority. In Denmark that is Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.dk). Email topshelfhockeytrivia@gmail.com and include your player ID if you can. To find it, ask us how and we will explain. We do not sell your data.

Children. The game is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Refund policy

  • You can ask for a full refund of unlimited mode within 14 days of buying it, for any reason. Email topshelfhockeytrivia@gmail.com with the email address you used at checkout.
  • If unlimited mode does not unlock after a successful payment and we cannot fix it, you get a full refund at any time.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.
  • If you are in the EU or UK, you normally have a 14-day right to cancel digital purchases. By buying unlimited mode you ask for it to be unlocked straight away. Where the law lets you give up that right once access begins, checkout will ask you to confirm that. Our 14-day refund promise above applies either way.
